Privacy Policy

Application: British Hills Highland Companion

Effective date: 1st November 2026

1. Overview

British Hills ("we," "our," or "us") operates the British Hills Highland Companion mobile application. This Privacy Policy informs users of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when using our application.

Key Takeaway: Your journal, photos, lesson notes and saved glossary are kept on your device. British Hills does not receive those records through the app. Dictionary lookups send the requested word or phrase to an external dictionary service, as explained in Section 4. The app contains no advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs.

2. Information Collection and Storage

The Highland Companion app stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Journal Entries & Photos: Memory text, selected or captured photos, frame settings and saved page layouts.
  • Lessons & Glossary: Lesson names, teacher names, notes, selected photos, vocabulary, definitions and written pronunciation symbols.
  • Application Preferences: Language selection, guest display name and completion of the introductory guide.
  • Reading & Archives: Unlocked lesson archive identifiers and previously opened Gazette articles.

The app does not provide guest accounts or its own cloud synchronisation. Words or phrases used for dictionary lookup are transmitted as described in Section 4. Device backups and Photos or iCloud synchronisation, if enabled, are controlled by your device settings and account provider.

3. Device Permissions

To enable specific interactive learning features, the application may request access to hardware components on your mobile device:

Camera Access

Used to take journal photos and scan lesson QR codes. QR codes are processed on the device, and captured journal photos are stored in the app. The app does not upload camera images or streams to British Hills or the dictionary provider.

Photo Library Access

Used to select photos for your journal and lesson workbooks and to save souvenir videos to Photos. Videos are assembled on the device. You can manage these permissions in your device settings; declining access prevents the related photo, scanning or video-saving feature. The app does not record microphone audio.

4. Dictionary Lookup and External Services

Dictionary lookup: The app uses Free Dictionary API at api.dictionaryapi.dev to retrieve English definitions, parts of speech and written pronunciation symbols. When you request a definition or save vocabulary from your Journal, Lessons or Gazette, the lookup word or phrase is sent to this provider over HTTPS. If a phrase is not found, the app may also look up individual words from it. The provider receives normal network information, including your IP address, in order to handle the request. The app does not attach your guest display name, photos, full journal entries or lesson workbooks to these requests. Please do not enter personal or sensitive information in vocabulary lookup fields. Internet access is required for new lookups; you can still enter and save vocabulary manually if a lookup is unavailable.

The dictionary provider operates independently of British Hills and controls its own processing and retention of request logs. Resetting or deleting this app does not delete information that a third-party provider may hold. See the provider's documentation above for information about its service.

Other connections: Spoken pronunciation uses the device's text-to-speech service. Opening the privacy policy or another web link connects to that website and its hosting or content providers. This policy page uses GitHub Pages, Google Fonts and the Tailwind CSS CDN. If you choose Send Feedback, your email application opens a message to British Hills; nothing is emailed until you send it, and any information you include is then handled by your email provider and British Hills to respond to your enquiry. There is no runtime connection to Gemini for the bundled background music.

No advertising or analytics: The app contains no advertising, tracking software or analytics SDKs, and does not track your activity across other apps or websites.

5. Children's Privacy

The app is an educational companion for Highland Life guests and does not require account registration or a date of birth. Children should use the app with appropriate guidance from a parent or guardian, particularly when entering vocabulary for external dictionary lookup or sending email feedback. Personal or sensitive information should not be entered into lookup fields. If you believe a child has included personal information in a message sent to British Hills, please contact us using the details below.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete individual journal entries, lesson workbooks and glossary words within the app. App-owned photos and page layouts are removed when no saved journal or lesson still references them. Settings > Reset Entire App clears your saved app records and app-owned media and restores the sample entries. Deleting the app (rather than offloading it) removes its local app data. Souvenir videos already saved to Photos, copies you have shared, and device or cloud backups are separate: manage or delete them through the relevant Photos, backup or sharing service. Dictionary-provider records and emails are not removed by an app reset.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
