Privacy Policy

Application: British Hills Highland Companion

1. Overview

British Hills ("we," "our," or "us") operates the British Hills Highland Companion mobile application. This Privacy Policy informs users of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when using our application.

Key Takeaway: The Highland Companion application is designed with user privacy as a core priority. We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, usage statistics, or telemetry from your device.

2. Information Collection and Storage

All data created, entered, or captured within the Highland Companion app is stored locally on your device. This includes:

  • Journal Entries & Photos: Scrapbook notes, photos selected from your library, and custom frame designs.
  • Lessons Diary & Glossary: Notes taken during classes, saved vocabulary words, and phonetic preferences.
  • Application Preferences: Language settings (English / Japanese) and guest profile display names.
  • Progress Data: Weekly challenge completion states and quiz results.

None of this data is ever uploaded to external servers, cloud storage services, or shared with third parties.

3. Device Permissions

To enable specific interactive learning features, the application may request access to hardware components on your mobile device:

Camera Access

Used strictly for scanning QR codes during lessons in British Hills classrooms. No images or camera streams are recorded or transmitted off-device.

Photo Library Access

Used solely to allow you to select photographs from your device to decorate your personal Journal and generate souvenir memory files locally.

4. Third-Party Analytics & Advertising

The Highland Companion application contains no third-party advertising, no tracking software, and no analytics SDKs (such as Google Analytics, Firebase, or Facebook SDK). We do not track your activity across other applications or websites.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application is designed for educational use by students and guests participating in the Highland Life immersion programme. Because we do not collect any personal information from any user, the application is fully compliant with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international data privacy laws.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all data is stored exclusively on your device, deleting or uninstalling the Highland Companion application will permanently delete all saved journal entries, glossary items, and lesson notes from your device. You retain 100% ownership and control over your data at all times.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
