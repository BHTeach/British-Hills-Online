British Hills Highland Companion

Highland Companion is your free companion app for the Highland Life course at British Hills. This guide explains how to record your stay, review lessons and continue learning at home. Interactive examples on this webpage are demonstrations only: they do not change the app or your saved records.

Module 02

2. The Journal & Souvenir Video

Save your memories from British Hills and practice writing in English.

Memory Pages & Frames

  • Personal Journal: Tap ADD NEW ENTRY, choose a prompt, write your memory and optionally add a photo using CAMERA or GALLERY. Tap SAVE to keep the page.
  • Photo Frames: Choose Polaroid, Victorian or Parchment for your photo. Pages without photos use the default empty-image design in souvenir videos.
  • Page limit: ADD NEW ENTRY is available while the Journal contains fewer than 10 pages. Lesson copies added to the Journal also appear in its page count.

British Spellings in Dictionary Definitions

Fetched dictionary definitions convert supported American spellings to British spellings, such as 'color' to 'colour'. Your journal writing stays as you typed it. The separate spelling demonstration below is a webpage example, not a Journal editor feature.

Souvenir Video

  • Souvenir Video: CREATE SOUVENIR VIDEO includes journal pages with or without photos, plus lesson pages added to the Journal.
  • Music & Cancellation: Turn BACKGROUND MUSIC on or off before starting. Keep the app open while the video is created. You can cancel during page preparation or recording; the final save to Photos must finish.
  • Save & Share: Allow Photos access to save the video. You can then open Photos and share the saved video with friends or family.

Module 03

3. Lessons Diary

Keep track of what you learn in every class to build your personal learning diary.

Class Records

  • Lesson Details: Use PREPARE NEW LESSON, enter the lesson and teacher names, then OPEN WORKBOOK.
  • Class Notes: Record your key takeaway, target phrases, new vocabulary and a comprehension rating from 1 to 5 stars.
  • Lesson Photo: Use UPDATE PHOTO to select a teacher or whiteboard photo from your device's gallery.
  • Keep Your Work: Tap SAVE LESSON. From the lesson list, ADD TO JOURNAL saves a copy of the workbook page in your Journal.

One-Tap Glossary Save

Save Vocabulary: Tap ADD TO GLOSSARY beside a word in NEW VOCABULARY to save it for review. The + buttons add another vocabulary or target-phrase field; they do not save an expression to the Glossary.

Module 04

4. The Glossary (Personal Dictionary)

Collect English vocabulary and definitions in your personal Glossary.

Saving Words and Looking Up Definitions

  • Save Words: Journal vocabulary is added when you save the entry. In Lessons, Gazette and Word of the Day, use the corresponding ADD TO GLOSSARY button.
  • Manual Entries: Use the Glossary's + button to enter a word and definition yourself, or request a dictionary lookup.
  • Online Dictionary: New lookups send the requested word or phrase to Free Dictionary API for English definitions, parts of speech and written pronunciation symbols. Internet access is required; a definition may be unavailable. You can enter a definition manually. See the privacy policy.
  • Spoken Pronunciation: Tap the speaker button to use your device's British English text-to-speech voice. Voice availability depends on the device.
  • Search & Delete: Search saved words and definitions, or delete words you no longer need.

Module 05

5. Practice & Weekly Challenges

Open the Learning tab for Word of the Day, vocabulary review and weekly language challenges.

Word of the Day

Daily Vocabulary: A word is selected each day from the collection bundled with the app, with a definition, pronunciation symbols and an example where available. Tap Add to Glossary to keep it.

Vocabulary Quizzes

Personal Vocabulary Review: Add at least 3 words to your Glossary before starting. The initial sample Glossary contains two; adding Word of the Day is one way to reach three. Review quizzes use definitions from your saved Glossary.

Weekly Highland Challenges

  • Part A • Politeness Ranking: Arrange phrases from Most Formal to Most Casual.
  • Part B • Fill-in-the-Blanks: Choose the word that completes a short text.
  • Part C • Pronunciation & Usage: Answer questions about British English sounds and language patterns.
  • Weekly Selection: Challenges rotate from the bundled collection according to the device's date.

Module 06

6. The Highland Gazette

Read weekly articles about British culture, history, and life at British Hills.

Weekly Reading Features

  • Weekly Reading: Read the current week's selection from the British Hills articles bundled with the app. Previously opened articles remain accessible.
  • Select a Word: Tap a word in the article to place it in the vocabulary field. This does not open a definition popup in the app.
  • Save & Review: Tap ADD TO GLOSSARY to save the word and request its definition, then open Glossary to review it. COPY copies the selected word to the clipboard.

Module 07

7. Settings & Profile

Use the header language control and Settings gear to personalise the app and manage local data. No account or password is required.

Language Switch

English / 日本語: The header control switches supported interface labels and explanations between English and Japanese. Some screens and English-learning materials remain in English; dictionary definitions are English.

Guest Name, Help & Privacy

Change Guest Name personalises the Learning greeting. The name is not automatically added to journal pages or souvenir videos. Settings also provides the App Guide, Send Feedback, Privacy Policy and Reset Entire App. Reset clears local records and app-owned media and restores the sample entries; videos already saved to Photos must be managed separately.
