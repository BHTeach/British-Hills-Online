1. The Archives (Supplementary Lesson Materials)

The Archives serve as your digital repository for interactive lesson content during your stay.

QR Code Scanner

In-Class Unlocking: In each lesson, scan the unique QR code provided by your teacher to instantly unlock exclusive supplementary study materials tailored to that specific class.

Interactive Activities

  • Vocabulary & Anagram Matchers: Unscramble terms and pair target words with contextual definitions to reinforce key lesson vocabulary.
  • Sentence Builders & Ordering: Reorder clause blocks to master authentic British sentence patterns and idiomatic structures.
  • Pronunciation & Phonetics: Practice syllable stress, linking sounds, intonation contours, and natural speech rhythm using interactive audio cues.
  • Special Lesson Modules: Access dedicated interactive tools designed for specific immersion classes, including digital card decks and magic trick simulators.

2. The Journal & Souvenir Video

Capture and preserve your personal Highland Life memories while practicing expressive English writing.

Memory Pages & Framing

  • Personal Scrapbook: Write reflections and attach photos captured around the British Hills estate.
  • Classic Photo Framing: Personalise your images by choosing between Polaroid, Victorian, or Parchment frame styles.

British Spelling Converter

Converts standard American spellings to British English (e.g., color to colour, traveler to traveller, center to centre).

Social Media Souvenir Video

  • Animated Keepsake: Automatically generate an animated, vertical souvenir video of your journal scrapbook complete with background music.
  • Direct Export: Save the video directly to your device's Camera Roll for easy sharing on social media.

3. Lessons Diary

Track what you study in every classroom session to build a diary of what you learned.

Classroom Records

  • Lesson Reflection: Record the lesson module, teacher name, date, and your personal comprehension rating (1 to 5 stars).
  • Whiteboard Capture: Snap and store photos of classroom whiteboards, worksheets, and teacher notes directly into the corresponding entry.
  • Key Takeaways & Target Phrases: Jot down essential grammar points, functional expressions, and new vocabulary introduced during class.

One-Tap Glossary Sync

Instant Saving: Tap the + button beside any highlighted expression or term in your notes to automatically transfer it to The Glossary for future review.

4. The Glossary (Personal Dictionary)

Your personal vocabulary book grows continuously alongside your learning progress.

Automatic Vocabulary Records

  • Centralised Collection: Terms saved from your Journal, Lessons Diary, Highland Gazette articles, or Word of the Day are automatically aggregated in your personal dictionary.
  • Automated Data Retrieval: The app automatically fetches English definitions, parts of speech, and IPA (International Phonetic Alphabet) transcriptions for every entry.
  • British Audio Pronunciation: Tap the speaker button beside any headword to hear natural British English pronunciation recorded by native speakers.
  • Search & Filter: Quickly find any word you have collected using the real-time filter engine.

5. Continuous Learning & Weekly Challenges

Keep learning after leaving British Hills through ongoing practice.

Word of the Day

Daily Vocabulary: Receive a curated daily English word complete with IPA phonetics, definitions, historical context, and real-world example sentences.

Dynamic Vocab Review

Personalised Quizzes: Take interactive multiple-choice quizzes generated dynamically from the words stored in your personal Glossary.

Weekly Highland Challenges

  • Part A • Politeness Ranking: Drag and arrange phrases from Most Formal to Most Casual to master British pragmatics and social etiquette.
  • Part B • Graded Cloze: Complete fill-in-the-blank reading passages calibrated to refine reading comprehension and contextual grammar.
  • Part C • Phonetics & Stress: Test your understanding of primary syllable stress, connected speech patterns, and silent letters across challenging headwords.

6. The Highland Gazette

Stay connected with British Hills through weekly articles exploring British and Commonwealth heritage, traditions, lifestyle, and history.

Weekly Reading Features

  • Weekly Articles: Read weekly articles published directly by British Hills instructors and staff.
  • Tap-to-Lookup: Tap any unfamiliar word within an article text block to open a floating definition card.
  • Record and Save: Tap Add to Glossary on the definition card to save the term directly to your personal dictionary.
