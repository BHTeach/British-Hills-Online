British Hills Highland Companion

1. The Archives (Extra Lesson Materials)

The Archives hold interactive study materials for your lessons at British Hills.

QR Code Scanner

Unlocking in Class: Scan the QR code from your teacher in each lesson to open special study materials for that class.

Interactive Activities

  • Word & Anagram Games: Unscramble words and match them with their meanings to learn new lesson words.
  • Sentence Building: Put word blocks in order to practice natural British sentences.
  • Pronunciation Practice: Listen and practice British pronunciation, word stress, and rhythm with audio samples.
  • Special Class Tools: Use interactive tools for special classes, like digital card games or magic trick activities.

Save your memories from British Hills and practice writing in English.

Changes American spelling to British spelling (for example, "color" to "colour" and "center" to "centre").

Souvenir Video

  • Short Memory Video: Automatically make a short vertical video of your journal photos with background music.

3. Lessons Diary

Keep track of what you learn in every class to build your personal learning diary.

Class Records

  • Lesson Notes: Save the lesson name, teacher name, date, and rate how well you understood the class (1 to 5 stars).
  • Useful Expressions & Grammar: Write down helpful grammar points, useful expressions, and new words from class.

One-Tap Glossary Save

Instant Save: Tap the + button next to any expression or word to save it directly to your Glossary for review later.

4. The Glossary (Personal Dictionary)

Your personal dictionary grows as you learn new English words.

Automatic Word Saver

  • All Words in One Place: Words saved from your Journal, Lessons Diary, Highland Gazette articles, or Word of the Day are automatically saved to your dictionary.
  • Automatic Word Info: The app automatically adds simple English definitions, word types (noun, verb, etc.), and pronunciation guides.
  • British Audio Pronunciation: Tap the speaker button next to any word to listen to its British English pronunciation.
  • Easy Search: Type in the search box to quickly find words in your dictionary.

5. Practice & Weekly Challenges

Keep practicing English after leaving British Hills.

Word of the Day

Daily Vocabulary: Learn a new English word every day with simple definitions, pronunciation guides, and example sentences.

Vocabulary Quizzes

Personalized Quizzes: Practice with quick multiple-choice quizzes created from the words in your Glossary.

Weekly Highland Challenges

  • Part A • Politeness Ranking: Put phrases in order from Most Formal to Most Casual to learn polite British English.
  • Part B • Fill-in-the-Blanks: Fill in missing words in short reading texts to practice reading and grammar.
  • Part C • Pronunciation & Stress: Practice word stress, silent letters, and British English speech patterns.

Part A Mini-Game: Politeness Ranking

Order: Most Formal (Top) to Casual (Bottom)

Put these three sentences in order from most polite to most casual:

"Give me a hand with this, will you?"
"I would be immensely grateful if you could assist me."
"Could you please help me with this?"

6. The Highland Gazette

Read weekly articles about British culture, history, and life at British Hills.

Weekly Reading Features

  • Weekly Articles: Read interesting weekly articles written by British Hills teachers.
  • Tap to Check Meaning: Tap any word you do not know in an article to see its meaning right away.
  • Save Words Easily: Tap "Add to Glossary" on the definition card to save the word to your personal dictionary.
